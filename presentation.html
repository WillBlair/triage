A Real Story

Meet Margaret, 67

😰

Before Triage

Margaret came in after a hypertensive crisis — BP 178/112. Her doctor had 11 minutes. He skimmed a 9-page discharge note, tried to recall ACE inhibitor contraindications for her CKD, and prescribed from memory.

Three weeks later, Margaret's potassium spiked. She felt weak, didn't think to call. Her daughter found her on the kitchen floor.

A preventable hospitalization. A terrified family. A doctor haunted by what he missed.

→
💚

With Triage

Same doctor. Same 11 minutes. He uploads the discharge note. In seconds, Triage flags her CKD, surfaces an ARB over an ACE inhibitor, and scores it 91/100 for her profile.

He selects it. The 8-week simulation immediately highlights hyperkalemia risk and sets a renal monitoring flag at week 3.

Two weeks in, Margaret's Discord check-in reports fatigue and muscle weakness. Triage flags it as a potential potassium issue — her doctor calls her that afternoon.

Margaret stays home. Her doctor sleeps at night.
